COURSE OUTCOMES: AI FOUNDATIONS COURSE
WRITTEN IN ASD-STE100 (SIMPLIFIED TECHNICAL ENGLISH)

1. GENERAL OUTCOME
- This course teaches you how to use artificial intelligence (AI) tools effectively.
- You will change from a passive AI user into an active workflow designer.
- You do not need programming or complex mathematics for this course.

2. OUTCOMES FOR BEGINNERS
- Understand basic AI concepts and models without technical jargon.
- Use AI tools with confidence for daily tasks.
- Overcome the fear of job loss caused by AI.
- Write messages and emails quickly.
- Summarize long documents and analyze data.

3. OUTCOMES FOR STUDENTS AND LEARNERS
- Use AI as a private tutor for continuous learning.
- Ask the AI to explain complex topics with simple examples.
- Make study materials from notes and recorded lectures.
- Make flashcards, summaries, and practice quizzes in seconds.
- Find your knowledge gaps and improve your test results.
- Get practical AI skills for future job opportunities.

4. OUTCOMES FOR CURRENT AI USERS
- Stop guessing when you write prompts.
- Apply a structured six-pillar framework to get reliable results.
- Use persona, context, task, instructions, format, and chain-of-thought rules.
- Manage context limits and connect external files to AI models.
- Build automated workflows with n8n.
- Run background pipelines to replace manual copy and paste tasks.
