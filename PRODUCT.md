# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students and professionals seeking practical AI literacy, tooling proficiency, and automated workflows in a workshop or self-paced course setting.

## Product Purpose
Interactive master presentation deck for the AI Foundations Course, deconstructing modern AI concepts, prompt engineering, and visual automation into practical, executable knowledge.

## Positioning
Applied, hands-on mental models and executable workflows (prompt craft, real-world tool stacks, n8n automation loops) prioritized over abstract mathematical theory.

## Operating Context
Live interactive classroom/workshop presentations and web-based slide deck reviews, built with Slidev, Vue 3, UnoCSS, and Vite.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Slidev interactive deck architecture (Markdown + Vue 3 components + UnoCSS + VueUse Motion).
- 5 Core Modules:
  1. The AI Landscape (Evolution, Ecosystem, ML/DL/LLMs)
  2. The Modern AI Toolbox (Chat, Research, Visuals, Coding)
  3. Speaking to the Machine (Prompt Engineering & Bot Mastery)
  4. Supercharging Personal Growth (AI as Tutor & Socratic Method)
  5. Putting It on Autopilot (n8n Automations, Workflows, Agents)
- Technical constraints: 16:9 aspect ratio, dark color scheme, presentation-mode keyboard & click navigations, no text overflows.

## Brand Commitments
- Tone: Pragmatic, modern, authoritative yet accessible, builder-centric.
- Aesthetics: High-craft interactive slides, diagrammatic cards, dark theme consistency.

## Evidence on Hand
- Complete course outlines and module outcomes (`course points.md`, `course_outcomes.txt`, `module_outcomes.txt`, `course_summary_ste.txt`).
- Active Slidev structure and custom Vue components (`CoverSlide`, `AboutInstructor`, `TimelineRoadmap`, `OutcomeBeginners`, `OutcomeStudents`, `customIcons.ts`).

## Product Principles
- Actionable over Academic: Focus on practical utility, tools, and automation over raw theory.
- Visual & Conceptual Clarity: Deconstruct complex abstractions into visual diagrams and progressive reveals.
- Rhythm & Restraint: High scannability, clear typography hierarchy, and deliberate slide pacing.
