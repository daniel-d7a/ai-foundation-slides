# Overcoming Plateaus: Automated Gap Analysis
How to use AI to find the blind spots in your knowledge before exams do.

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4 mt-4 text-xs">
  <div class="p-4 rounded-xl bg-slate-900/80 border border-slate-700/60">
    <div class="font-bold text-sm text-white mb-2">The 3-Step Gap Diagnostic Prompt:</div>
    <ol class="space-y-2 text-slate-300">
      <li><strong>1. Test Me:</strong> "Generate a 5-question conceptual quiz on [Topic] requiring non-trivial reasoning."</li>
      <li><strong>2. Answer Honestly:</strong> Submit your reasoning without looking up external notes.</li>
      <li><strong>3. Feed Misses Back:</strong> "Here are my incorrect answers. Diagnose my underlying misconception and explain the root logic."</li>
    </ol>
  </div>

  <div class="p-4 rounded-xl bg-indigo-950/20 border border-indigo-500/30 flex flex-col justify-between">
    <div>
      <div class="text-xs font-mono text-indigo-400 uppercase font-bold mb-1">Pedagogical Value</div>
      <p class="text-slate-200 leading-relaxed text-xs">
        Traditional studying fails because students re-read what they already know. Feeding your incorrect answers directly into an LLM isolates the exact cognitive gap in minutes.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="mt-3 text-[11px] text-emerald-300 font-mono">
      🎯 Targets high-leverage weaknesses directly
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
