# The AI Tutor Mindset Shift
Using AI as a personalized cognitive sparring partner rather than a shortcut oracle.

  <div v-click class="p-5 rounded-xl bg-slate-900/90 border-2 border-red-500/40 flex flex-col justify-between h-72">

        <span class="text-xs font-mono font-bold text-red-400 uppercase">THE PASSIVE PATHWAY</span>
        <span class="text-[10px] font-mono bg-red-500/20 text-red-300 px-2 py-0.5 rounded font-bold">ZERO RETENTION</span>
      </div>
      <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-white font-serif min-h-[2.5rem] flex items-center">The Oracle Mindset ("Give me the code")</h3>
      <p class="text-xs text-slate-300 mt-2 leading-relaxed font-sans">
        Prompting for direct answers and pasting them into homework or tickets. Creates immediate illusion of productivity while bypassing all synaptic wiring.
      </p>
      <ul class="text-[11px] text-slate-300 mt-3 space-y-1 font-mono">
        <li>• Zero active retrieval practice</li>
        <li>• Creates immediate dependency on the tool</li>
        <li>• Total failure during closed-book interviews &amp; exams</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pt-2 border-t border-red-900/40 text-xs font-mono text-red-400">
      Outcome: Cognitive atrophy &amp; surface familiarity
    </div>
  </div>

  <div v-click class="p-5 rounded-xl bg-slate-900/90 border-2 border-emerald-500/40 flex flex-col justify-between h-72">
    <div>
      <div class="flex items-center justify-between mb-2">
        <span class="text-xs font-mono font-bold text-emerald-400 uppercase">THE ACCELERATED PATHWAY</span>
        <span class="text-[10px] font-mono bg-emerald-500/20 text-emerald-300 px-2 py-0.5 rounded font-bold">10X RETENTION</span>
      </div>
      <h3 class="text-lg font-bold text-white font-serif min-h-[2.5rem] flex items-center">The Socratic Coach ("Guide my reasoning")</h3>
      <p class="text-xs text-slate-300 mt-2 leading-relaxed font-sans">
        Instructing the AI to question your deductions, challenge subtle misconceptions, and force you to derive the underlying mathematical mechanisms.
      </p>
      <ul class="text-[11px] text-slate-300 mt-3 space-y-1 font-mono">
        <li>• Relentless active recall testing</li>
        <li>• Rapid diagnosis of conceptual blind spots</li>
        <li>• Converts textbook theory into first-principles mastery</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div class="pt-2 border-t border-emerald-900/40 text-xs font-mono text-emerald-300">
      Outcome: Autonomous problem-solving capability
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
